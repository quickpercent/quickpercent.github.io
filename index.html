Free Easy Grader Calculator

Our free Easy Grader Calculator is an online EZ Grader that turns total questions and wrong answers into correct answers, percentages, letter grades, and pass-or-fail results. It is useful for tests, quizzes, exams, homework, practice papers, and classroom grading.

Teachers can use the calculator to grade papers quickly, while students and tutors can check test scores without doing repeated manual calculations. Choose a standard, plus-minus, or custom grading scale and generate a printable grading chart.

What Is an Easy Grader or EZ Grader?

An Easy Grader is a grading reference and test grade calculator that makes scoring faster. You enter the total number of questions and the number of wrong answers. The calculator finds the correct answers, converts the score into a percentage, and matches it to the selected grading scale.

You may also see this type of tool called an EZ Grader Calculator, Easy Grading Calculator, Quiz Grade Calculator, Test Score Calculator, or Letter Grade Calculator. The purpose is to make test scoring simple and consistent.

How to Use the Easy Grader Calculator

  1. Enter the total number of questions on the test.
  2. Enter the number of wrong answers.
  3. Choose Standard, Plus-Minus, or Custom grading.
  4. Select the passing percentage used by your class or school.
  5. Allow half points if partial-credit wrong answers are needed.
  6. Click Calculate Grade to view the score and grading chart.

Easy Grader Formula

The basic formula is Percentage = (Correct Answers ÷ Total Questions) × 100. Correct answers are calculated as Total Questions − Wrong Answers.

For example, if a test has 25 questions and 5 wrong answers, there are 20 correct answers. The score is 20 ÷ 25 × 100 = 80%. The letter grade depends on the grading scale selected.

Easy Grader Examples

On a 10-question test, 1 wrong answer gives 9 correct answers and a score of 90%. On a 20-question test, 2 wrong answers also gives 90%. On a 50-question test, 5 wrong answers gives 90% as well.

The total number of questions matters because the same number of mistakes can produce different percentages. Three wrong answers on a 10-question test gives 70%, while three wrong answers on a 100-question test gives 97%.

Easy Grader Chart

The built-in Easy Grader Chart shows wrong answers, correct answers, percentage, letter grade, and pass/fail status for the selected test size. The chart is especially useful when grading multiple papers with the same number of questions. You can also print the chart as a reference sheet.

Standard and Plus-Minus Grading Scales

Different schools and instructors use different grading policies. The Standard scale commonly uses A, B, C, D, and F. The Plus-Minus scale adds grades such as A+, A−, B+, B−, and C+. Because grading policies vary, this calculator also includes a Custom scale so you can enter your own percentage thresholds.

What Grade Is 90%, 80%, or 70%?

On the Standard scale used by this calculator, 90% or higher is A, 80%–89% is B, 70%–79% is C, 60%–69% is D, and below 60% is F. You can change the scale if your school uses different thresholds.

Easy Grader vs. Manual Grade Calculation

Manual grading requires subtracting mistakes, calculating the percentage, and checking the correct letter-grade range. An online Easy Grader combines these steps into one workflow, reducing repetitive arithmetic and making it easier to check many papers with the same test size.

Important Note About Grading Policies

Letter-grade boundaries are not universal. A school, teacher, college, or testing program may use its own grading rules. Always compare the calculator's selected scale with the grading policy that applies to your assessment.
