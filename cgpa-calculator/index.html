Standard CGPA Formula

The core standard formula used by universities is: CGPA = Total Quality Points Earned ÷ Total Credit Hours Completed.

Semester Quality Points = Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours.

CGPA Calculator Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) measures academic performance for a single semester or term. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) measures your overall average across all completed semesters combined.

2. How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Different universities follow different conversion rules. In standard 4.0 systems, multiplying your CGPA by 25 gives an approximate percentage (e.g., 3.2 CGPA × 25 = 80%).

3. Why do credit hours matter in calculating CGPA?

Semesters with more credit hours carry higher weight in your overall CGPA. For instance, a semester with 18 credits impacts your overall CGPA significantly more than a semester with 12 credits.

4. What is considered a good CGPA for graduation?

A CGPA of 3.0 or higher is generally considered good for graduation, while a CGPA of 3.5+ often qualifies students for honors, awards, and graduate school admission.
