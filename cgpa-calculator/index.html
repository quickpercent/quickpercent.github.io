Free Online CGPA Calculator

Our CGPA Calculator provides a simple way to combine your semester GPAs and credit hours to calculate your overall Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA). Perfect for university, college, and high school students track academic progress over multiple terms.

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents the weighted average of all your individual semester GPAs across your entire degree program or academic history.

How to Calculate CGPA

Calculating CGPA manually requires taking into account the total credit hours earned in each semester. Follow these steps using our tool:

  1. Enter your semester name or leave the default names.
  2. Enter the GPA earned in each individual semester.
  3. Enter the total credit hours completed in each semester.
  4. Click Calculate CGPA to get your total weighted cumulative average.

CGPA Calculation Formula

The standard formula for Cumulative GPA is: CGPA = Total Quality Points from All Semesters ÷ Total Credit Hours across All Semesters.

Quality points for a semester are calculated by: Semester Points = Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours.

CGPA Calculator FAQs

1. What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA measures academic performance for a single semester or term. CGPA measures cumulative overall performance across all completed semesters combined.

2. How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Different universities have different rules. In many standard systems, multiplying your 4.0 scale CGPA by 25 gives an approximate percentage (e.g., 3.0 CGPA × 25 = 75%).

3. Why do credit hours matter in CGPA?

Semesters with more credit hours carry a higher weight in your final CGPA calculation. For instance, a semester with 18 credits impacts your CGPA more than a semester with 12 credits.

4. What is a good CGPA for graduation?

A CGPA of 3.0 or higher is generally considered good. A CGPA above 3.5 usually qualifies for academic honors such as Cum Laude or Distinction.
