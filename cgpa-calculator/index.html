Standard CGPA Formula

The core standard formula used by universities is: CGPA = Total Quality Points Earned ÷ Total Credit Hours Completed.

Semester Quality Points = Semester GPA × Semester Credit Hours.

CGPA Calculator Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) measures academic performance for a single semester or term. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) measures your cumulative overall average across all completed semesters combined.

2. How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

In standard 4.0 grading systems, multiplying your CGPA by 25 gives an approximate percentage (e.g., 3.2 CGPA × 25 = 80%). Some Indian and international university systems use custom formulas like (CGPA × 9.5).

3. Why do credit hours matter in calculating CGPA?

Semesters with more total credit hours carry higher mathematical weight in your CGPA calculation. For instance, a 18-credit semester influences your overall CGPA more significantly than a 12-credit semester.

4. What formula does this CGPA calculator use?

The calculator uses the standard formula: CGPA = Total Quality Points (Sum of [Semester GPA × Semester Credits]) ÷ Total Credit Hours Completed.

5. What is considered a good CGPA for university graduation?

A CGPA of 3.0 or higher is generally considered good for college graduation, while a CGPA of 3.5+ qualifies students for academic honors, awards, and competitive master's/PhD admissions.
