The Standard 4.0 GPA Scale Chart

Most high schools and colleges in the US and internationally use the unweighted 4.0 GPA scale to evaluate academic performance:

  • A (4.0 Points): 90% – 100% (Excellent)
  • A- (3.7 Points): 87% – 89%
  • B+ (3.3 Points): 83% – 86%
  • B (3.0 Points): 80% – 82% (Above Average)
  • B- (2.7 Points): 77% – 79%
  • C+ (2.3 Points): 73% – 76%
  • C (2.0 Points): 70% – 72% (Average)
  • D (1.0 Point): 60% – 69% (Below Average)
  • F (0.0 Points): Below 60% (Failing Grade)

Formula Used by Our Semester GPA Calculator

The calculation uses the standard formula: GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Attempted Credit Hours.

For example, if you earn an 'A' (4.0) in a 3-credit class, your grade points for that class equal 4.0 × 3 = 12 total grade points.

GPA Calculator Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between an unweighted and weighted GPA calculator?

An unweighted GPA calculator evaluates all classes on a strict 0.0 to 4.0 scale regardless of subject difficulty. A weighted GPA calculator factors in advanced courses like AP, IB, or Honors classes, scaling up to 5.0 points to reward higher academic rigors.

2. How do credit hours affect my college GPA calculation?

Credit hours act as weights in your GPA formula. Courses with higher credit hours (e.g., 4 credits) have a larger impact on your overall semester GPA than courses with lower credit hours (e.g., 1 or 2 credits).

3. What is the standard formula used to calculate semester GPA?

The standard formula is: GPA = Total Earned Grade Points ÷ Total Attempted Credit Hours. Total grade points are calculated by multiplying each grade's numeric point value by its course credit hours.

4. What is considered a good college GPA?

A GPA of 3.0 (B average) or higher is generally considered good in college. A GPA of 3.5 or above is typically required for Dean's List recognition, academic honors, and competitive graduate school admissions.

5. What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) measures your performance for a single semester or term, whereas CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) measures your overall academic average across all completed terms combined.

6. Can I use this tool as a high school GPA calculator?

Yes, this tool works for high school students using the standard 4.0 grading scale to compute term and semester averages.

7. Is this GPA calculator free to use?

Yes, this online GPA calculator is 100% free with unlimited course additions and no registration required.
