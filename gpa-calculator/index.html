Free Online GPA Calculator

Our free GPA Calculator helps high school, college, and university students calculate their Grade Point Average (GPA) accurately for any term or semester. Enter your course grades and credit hours to generate your calculated GPA instantly.

How to Calculate Your GPA

Calculating your GPA requires multiplying your grade point values by credit hours. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Enter your course name (optional).
  2. Select your earned letter grade for each course.
  3. Enter the credit hours assigned to each course.
  4. Click Calculate GPA to get your instant results.

GPA Calculation Formula

The core standard GPA formula is: GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours.

Course Grade Points are calculated by: Course Points = Grade Scale Value × Credit Hours.

Standard 4.0 Scale Reference

  • A (4.0 Points): Excellent Performance (90% - 100%)
  • B (3.0 Points): Above Average (80% - 89%)
  • C (2.0 Points): Average Performance (70% - 79%)
  • D (1.0 Point): Below Average (60% - 69%)
  • F (0.0 Points): Failing Grade (Below 60%)

GPA Calculator FAQs

1. What is a GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a standard numerical score representing academic performance on a 0.0 to 4.0 scale.

2. How do credit hours affect my GPA?

Courses with higher credit hours carry greater weight in calculating your final GPA. Getting an A in a 4-credit course raises your GPA more than an A in a 1-credit course.

3. What is considered a good GPA?

Generally, a GPA of 3.0 or higher is considered good in high school and college, while 3.5+ often qualifies for honors and scholarships.

4. What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA reflects performance in a single semester or term, whereas CGPA (Cumulative GPA) measures overall performance across all completed semesters.

5. Can I use this calculator for high school and college?

Yes! This GPA calculator uses the universal standard 4.0 grading scale used by most high schools, colleges, and universities.
