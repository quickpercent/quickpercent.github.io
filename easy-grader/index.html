Free Easy Grader Calculator

Our free Easy Grader Calculator is a simple way to turn the number of questions and wrong answers on a test, quiz, or exam into a percentage, correct-answer count, letter grade, and pass-or-fail result. What Is an Easy Grader?

An Easy Grader is a grading reference and calculator that makes test scoring faster. You enter the total number of questions and the number of wrong answers. The tool determines how many answers were correct, calculates the percentage, and matches the percentage to the selected grading scale. You may also see this type of tool called an EZ Grader, EZ Grader Calculator, Test Grade Calculator, or Quiz Grade Calculator.

How Does the EZ Grader Work?

The process is straightforward: enter total questions, enter wrong answers, select a grading scale, and hit calculate. The percentage calculation uses the formula: Percentage = (Correct Answers ÷ Total Questions) × 100.

Easy Grader Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an Easy Grader?

An Easy Grader is an online calculator and reference tool that converts total test questions and wrong answers into correct answers, percentages, letter grades, and pass-or-fail results.

2. What is an EZ Grader?

EZ Grader is a widely used name for quick grading tools designed for teachers, students, and tutors to score test papers and quizzes without manual calculations.

3. How do I calculate a test grade from wrong answers?

Subtract wrong answers from total questions to find correct answers. Divide correct answers by total questions and multiply by 100 to get the score percentage.

4. What is the formula used by the Easy Grader Calculator?

The percentage formula is (Correct Answers ÷ Total Questions) × 100, where Correct Answers = Total Questions - Wrong Answers.

5. Can I use half points for grading?

Yes. Simply enable the "Allow half points (0.5)" checkbox to calculate partial deductions like 1.5 or 2.5 wrong answers.

6. Is this Easy Grader Calculator free to use?

Yes, this online EZ Grader calculator is completely free to use on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without registration.
