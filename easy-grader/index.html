Easy Grader Calculator

Easy Grader is a free online EZ Grader calculator for teachers, students and anyone who needs to calculate test grades quickly. Enter the total number of questions and wrong answers to instantly calculate the correct answers, percentage, letter grade and pass/fail status.

You can use Standard grading, Plus/Minus grading or create your own Custom grading scale. Half-point scoring is also supported.

How to Use Easy Grader

Enter the total number of questions, enter the number of wrong answers, select your grading scale and click Calculate Grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Easy Grader?

An Easy Grader is a grading tool that quickly converts test scores into percentages and letter grades.

What is an EZ Grader?

EZ Grader is another common name for an easy grading calculator used to calculate test scores.

Can I use decimal wrong answers?

Yes. This calculator supports manual decimal values such as 2.5, 3.5 and 4.5.

Can I create a custom grading scale?

Yes. Select Custom and enter your own minimum percentages for A, B, C, D and F.
