What Is an Easy Grader?

An Easy Grader, also known as an EZ Grader, helps teachers and students quickly convert the number of correct and incorrect answers into a percentage and letter grade.

How to Use the Easy Grader

Enter the total number of questions, enter the number of wrong answers, and click Calculate Grade. The calculator instantly shows the correct answers, percentage, letter grade, and result.

Easy Grader Formula

Correct Answers = Total Questions − Wrong Answers

Percentage = (Correct Answers ÷ Total Questions) × 100

Standard Grading Scale

A = 90–100%

B = 80–89%

C = 70–79%

D = 60–69%

F = Below 60%
